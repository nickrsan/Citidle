# Summary Report - 2026-05-07 20:43

## Input Prompt
Could you please modify the research display with a few changes?

1. When the user has insufficient funds to purchase an item, please fade that item out or otherwise indicate to the user that they can't purchase it.
2. When a research item has multiple levels that can be purchased, please add a progress bar onto the research item showing how many of that research item the user has purchased of the total levels available
3. When a research item depends on purchasing another item, please add that text onto the button too. When it depends on multiple levels of an item, note how many must be purchased. E.g. "Requires Residential Efficiency Level 3"

## Understanding
The goal was to enhance the Research UI with better visual feedback for affordability, progress, and prerequisites.
1. **Affordability**: Items that the user cannot afford (but are unlocked) should be visually distinct (faded).
2. **Progress**: Multi-level research items should display a progress bar.
3. **Prerequisites**: Research items should explicitly list their requirements (other research items and their levels).
